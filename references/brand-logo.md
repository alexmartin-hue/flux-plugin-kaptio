# Logo

The Kaptio wordmark is our primary identifier. The pink accent dot is our signature —
it should always be present unless space constraints require its removal.

## Primary — Dark

## Reversed — Light

## Without Accent Dot

Only when space is extremely constrained (favicons, small UI elements).

## Specifications
Aspect Ratio

91 : 29

Wordmark Color

#032E36 (dark) / #FFFFFF (light)

Accent Dot

#DE37A4

Minimum Height

16px

Clear Space

Height of the dot on all sides

File Formats

SVG (preferred), PNG @2x

## Don't
x Rotate or skew the wordmark

x Change the accent dot color

x Add drop shadows or effects

x Place on busy backgrounds without contrast

x Stretch or compress the proportions

x Use the dot as a standalone icon (except favicon)
