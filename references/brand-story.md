# Our Story

Kaptio exists to give travel companies the technology they need to sell, operate, and grow without compromise.

We power the world's leading multi-day travel operators, from group touring to independent travel, from cruise to rail journeys. Our customers create experiences people remember for a lifetime. Our role is to ensure the technology behind those experiences is just as exceptional.

Headquartered in Reykjavik, Iceland, with teams across Europe, North America, and APAC, and partners worldwide, we bring a truly global perspective to the travel industry.

Our purpose is clear

Powering the future of travel experiences.

## 💡 What We Stand For

Travel technology should support the people creating the experience, not slow them down.

Serve travellers, not spreadsheets.

We believe:

🤝 Work together

Great travel experiences are built by connected teams. Technology should bring people together, not create silos.

✊ Own it

Systems should create clarity and accountability, giving teams confidence in every step of the journey.

🔄 Keep improving

Travel is constantly evolving, and technology must evolve with it. Progress matters more than perfection.

⚡ Lead with energy

The best businesses move with purpose, momentum, and optimism. Technology should enable that pace, not restrict it.

## 🎯 Our Position

Kaptio is the operating platform for multi-day travel.

We provide modern travel technology that simplifies the selling and operating of complex travel experiences. By connecting sales, operations, and delivery in one flexible ecosystem, we give operators the control and clarity they need to scale.

We focus on doing one thing exceptionally well: Powering multi-day travel businesses to grow with confidence.
