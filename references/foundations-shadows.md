# Shadows

Four elevation levels, tinted with the Kaptio primary teal for brand-consistent depth.
Shadows use `rgba(3, 46, 54, ...)` — derived from `--flux-primary-800`.

Small

--flux-shadow-sm

Subtle elevation. Buttons, inputs.

Medium

--flux-shadow-md

Default card shadow. Most containers.

Large

--flux-shadow-lg

Prominent elevation. Dropdowns, popovers.

Extra Large

--flux-shadow-xl

Modal, overlay elevation.

## Border Radius

Six radius values from subtle rounding to full pills.

sm 0.25rem   md 0.5rem   lg 0.75rem   xl 1rem   2xl 1.5rem   full 9999px
