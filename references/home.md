# Flux
The design system for Kaptio. Tokens, typography, color, voice, and product identity — one source of truth for everything we build.

flux.kaptio.com

## Design Principles

### 🤝 Professional & Approachable
We balance business sophistication with warmth. Travel is personal — our design respects both the operator and their customer.

### ✨ Clean & Purposeful
Every element earns its place. No decorative noise, no gratuitous gradients. If it's on screen, it serves a function.

### 📊 Data-Driven
Travel operations are complex. Our design makes dense information scannable, comparable, and actionable.

### 🚀 Modern
Contemporary aesthetics with smooth, intentional interactions. We move forward — our interfaces should feel like it.
