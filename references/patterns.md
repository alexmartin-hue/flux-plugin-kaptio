# Patterns

Patterns describe how users move through experiences — entering a focused
outcome, completing their work, and returning to their broader context.
Unlike components, patterns are composed of multiple elements working together
to solve a UX problem.

[Core Pattern

Salesforce ↔ Edge Transitions

How users move between Salesforce and Edge apps — entering a focused
outcome like building a proposal or collecting a payment, and returning
to their broader context. The technology boundary is invisible to the user.](/patterns/edge-transitions)
