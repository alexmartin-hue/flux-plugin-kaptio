# Product Architecture

Kaptio's platform is modular — four layers, each with its own identity accent
built on shared Flux foundations. Every product inherits the core palette and typography,
then adds its own colour signature.

## Layers
[Core LayerFoundation of everything

The core travel platform. CRM, inventory, pricing, rules, suppliers.

→](/products/core)[Product ExperienceTailored to how you sell

Purpose-built for your business model. Voyage, Circle, Quest.

→](/products)[EdgeGo to market

Complete go-to-market platform. Documents, payments, e-commerce.

→](/products/edge)[AdvancedExtend & innovate

Next-generation capabilities. Integrations, analytics, AI agents.
